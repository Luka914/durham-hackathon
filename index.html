Quick setup · one walkthrough video

Walk from an entrance and briefly point at room signs. We’ll detect sign text, order stops by video, and sketch a connected route. No room names, coordinates, or links to type.

Privacy: The app processes video locally; it does not send or save the footage. OCR text is used temporarily to suggest stop names, not stored wholesale. Edit or remove suggestions that contain personal information; only names you confirm and route points are saved. Avoid filming people where possible.     Teach camera recognition with sign photos

    Choose a mapped stop and upload a sharp, close-up sign photo. The app enlarges it and tries contrast and black-and-white versions; select the clearly shown wording below. Camera scans and walkthroughs can then match that sign to this stop.

    Privacy: Photos are read locally and discarded. OCR suggestions start unchecked; only text you select or type and save is kept. Don’t select people’s names or other personal information.
    Upload sign photos to see OCR suggestions. Select the matching text, or type it below if OCR misses it.

    Optional · GPS pin and public map lookup
    Building GPS pin: Save an approximate GPS location while at the building. GPS can identify the building, not a room or floor. The pin is stored in this browser; when you request a public map lookup, approximate coordinates are sent to the OpenStreetMap Overpass service.
    Look for public OpenStreetMap building features near the saved GPS pin. Import results are suggestions; review and connect them before navigation. Indoor room data may not be mapped. © OpenStreetMap contributors.
